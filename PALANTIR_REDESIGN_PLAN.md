# NexFlow Enterprise → Palantir-Style Redesign Plan

Transform the enterprise landing from "warm wellness B2B" to "workforce infrastructure power platform."

---

## Current vs. Target Vibe

| Element | Current NexFlow | Target (Palantir-style) |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Background | Warm cream `#fcf6ef` | Dark/black `#0a0a0a` or stark white sections |
| Text | Soft taupe `#94877c` | High contrast white/black |
| Accent | Forest green `#1F4D3A` | Keep green but bolder usage |
| Tone | Friendly, wellness | Commanding, infrastructure |
| Headlines | Elegant, italic | Bold, declarative, short |
| CTAs | "Apply Now" | "Request a Demo →" / "Start Building →" |

## Key Palantir Design Principles to Adopt

1. **Declarative, not descriptive** - "Automate operations" not "We help you automate"
2. **Numbered taxonomy** - Shows structured thinking
3. **Social proof density** - Many testimonials, scrolling
4. **Infrastructure language** - "Platform", "OS", "System", "Deploy"
5. **Arrow CTAs** - "Get Started →" implies forward momentum
6. **Selective positioning** - "Not for everyone" = premium
7. **Dark confidence** - High contrast, bold statements
